## What I build

**The Moves Playbooks**, a trilogy that uses Gomoku as the training method for thinking:

- **Story Moves**: creative thinking, ages 10 to 12. [link when live]
- **World Moves**: critical thinking, ages 16 to 18. [link when live]
- **Five Moves**: strategic thinking, working adults. [link when live]
- **The Moves Playbooks Journal**: the full-colour companion. [link when live]
- **Five in a Row**: the free app where the five moves are practised. [link when live]

The five moves: Scan the whole board · Spot the open three · Block the four first · Watch for the double threat · Make the forcing move.
